Dizko Events Connector Terms

Last updated: June 9, 2026

These terms apply to the Dizko Events connector and MCP service. The connector helps people discover live event listings and create opt-in preference profiles for personalized recommendations.

Event Information

Event listings, prices, availability, venues, times, ticket links, and source links can change. Always confirm final details with the event organizer, venue, ticketing provider, or linked source before traveling, buying tickets, or making plans.

Third-Party Links

The connector may return links to Dizko pages, event organizers, venues, ticketing providers, or other event sources. Third-party services are governed by their own terms and policies. Dizko does not control third-party ticket inventory, refunds, age restrictions, entry rules, or venue policies.

Ticket purchase tools can prepare offers and locked quotes, but autonomous purchase requires explicit written confirmation and an integrated purchase provider. Third-party-only ticket links are checkout handoff, not proof that Dizko or an agent purchased a ticket. Do not use the connector to bypass checkout controls, CAPTCHAs, account requirements, payment verification, age restrictions, or venue/ticketing rules.

Preference Memory

Preference memory is optional. The assistant should ask for consent before creating or updating a saved preference profile. Deletion of saved Dizko connector preferences and feedback requires the private profile secret and explicit confirmation.

Acceptable Use

Do not use the connector to abuse, disrupt, reverse engineer, scrape at scale, bypass access controls, or violate third-party service terms. Do not submit another person's profile secret or private data.

No Emergency Or Safety-Critical Use

The connector is for event discovery and planning. It is not designed for emergency, legal, medical, safety, transportation, or venue-access decisions where inaccurate or delayed information could create harm.

Privacy And Support

See the privacy policy for data handling, retention, and deletion details. For help, email support@urbanplayground.xyz.
