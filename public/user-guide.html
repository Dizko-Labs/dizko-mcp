Dizko Events Connector User Guide

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Dizko Events is Dizko's connector for helping ChatGPT, Claude, and other MCP-compatible assistants find current concerts, club nights, festivals, cultural events, and night-out options from live Dizko event inventory across 47 cities, with every time shown in the city's local timezone.

Connect

Add this production MCP endpoint in ChatGPT Developer Mode, Claude custom connectors, or another remote-MCP compatible client:

https://mcp.dizko.app/mcp

Per-client steps are at www.dizko.app/mcp/install. No account or API key is needed for event discovery.

Useful Prompts

  • Find five techno events in Berlin this weekend.
  • What's on in London tonight under 20 pounds?
  • Plan a night out in New York with a concert first and a late party fallback.
  • Give me a Dizko roundup of everything happening in Berlin today.
  • How busy is Berlin this week, and which venues have the most going on?
  • Who is Nina Kraviz and when does she play next?
  • What's on at Berghain this month?
  • Which cities does Dizko cover?
  • Ask what kind of events I generally like and remember my preferences if I consent.
  • I went to that event yesterday; I liked the music but not the crowd.
  • Delete my Dizko saved event preferences and feedback history.

A request that names a city and a timeframe is a single search. The assistant may ask about event type, vibe, budget, or area first, but only when the request is genuinely ambiguous.

Preference Memory

Preference memory is optional. The assistant should ask what kinds of events, genres, vibes, budgets, areas, venues, promoters, artists to follow, and avoidances you generally like. It should ask for consent before saving anything.

If you consent, the connector creates a private preference profile with a profile_id (starting dzk_) and one-time profile_secret (starting dzs_). Future personalized recommendations, feedback, preference reads, updates, and deletion require both values. The service stores only a hash of the secret and cannot reveal the raw secret later.

Saved taste ranks results; it never hides events. You can also set per-weekday rules, for example techno on Fridays but something quieter on Sundays.

Learning From Feedback

After an event, the assistant can ask whether you went, whether you liked it, what to remember, and an optional rating. Feedback is saved only when you provide a liked/disliked answer, rating, or notes. A liked event promotes its genres, vibes, event types, venue, and promoters. A disliked event steers future ranking away from that venue and promoter; its genres are only penalized when your notes blame the music, and a genre needs two negative signals before it becomes an avoid rule. Notes such as "liked the music," "too crowded," "too expensive," or "too late" become learned preference or avoid signals. Anything you saved as a preference is never learned against you.

Deleting Saved Preferences

You can ask the assistant to delete your saved Dizko connector preferences and feedback history. Deletion requires your profile_id, private profile_secret, and explicit confirmation that the deletion is scoped to Dizko connector memory.

Limits

Event details can change. Confirm times, age restrictions, ticket availability, entry rules, prices, refunds, and venue policies with the linked event source, organizer, ticketing provider, or venue before making plans.

Ticket purchase safety

The connector can show ticket offers, create signed quotes that expire after ten minutes, and ask for explicit written confirmation before purchase. Third-party ticket links return checkout handoff unless an integrated purchase provider is configured. Autonomous purchase is only available within a confirmed quote through an integrated provider such as Hermes, OpenClaw, Dizko Checkout, a partner API, or delegated payment.

Do not confirm a purchase unless the event, venue, date, quantity, ticket type, max total, and refund constraints are correct. Your confirmation must say "buy" or "purchase" and repeat the quantity and max total. If price, date, venue, ticket type, quantity, or terms change, the assistant should stop and ask again.
