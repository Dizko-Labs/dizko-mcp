Dizko Events Connector User Guide

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Dizko Events is Dizko's connector for helping ChatGPT, Claude, and other MCP-compatible assistants find current concerts, club nights, festivals, cultural events, and night-out options from live Dizko event inventory.

Connect

Add this verified production MCP endpoint in ChatGPT Developer Mode, Claude custom connectors, or another remote-MCP compatible client:

https://mcp.dizko.app/mcp

Do not use https://mcp.urbanplayground.xyz/mcp yet. That branded domain is reserved for later and is not verified for review or public traffic.

Useful Prompts

  • Find five techno events in Berlin this weekend.
  • Find something good tonight, but ask me what kind of event and vibe first.
  • Plan a night out in New York with a concert first and a late party fallback.
  • Ask what kind of events I generally like and remember my preferences if I consent.
  • I went to that event yesterday; I liked the music but not the crowd.
  • Delete my Dizko saved event preferences and feedback history.

Preference Memory

Preference memory is optional. The assistant should ask what kinds of events, genres, vibes, budgets, areas, venues, and avoidances you generally like. It should ask for consent before saving anything.

If you consent, the connector creates a private preference profile with a profile_id and one-time profile_secret. Future personalized recommendations, feedback, preference reads, updates, and deletion require both values. The service stores only a hash of the secret and cannot reveal the raw secret later.

Learning From Feedback

After an event, the assistant can ask whether you went, whether you liked it, what to remember, and an optional rating. Feedback is saved only when you provide a liked/disliked answer, rating, or notes. Positive feedback can improve matching for similar genres, vibes, event types, and venues. Notes such as "liked the music," "too crowded," "too expensive," or "too late" can become learned preference or avoid signals. Negative feedback can create avoid signals for future recommendations.

Deleting Saved Preferences

You can ask the assistant to delete your saved Dizko connector preferences and feedback history. Deletion requires your profile_id, private profile_secret, and explicit confirmation that the deletion is scoped to Dizko connector memory.

Limits

Event details can change. Confirm times, age restrictions, ticket availability, entry rules, prices, refunds, and venue policies with the linked event source, organizer, ticketing provider, or venue before making plans.

Ticket purchase safety

The connector can show ticket offers, create locked quotes, and ask for explicit written confirmation before purchase. Third-party ticket links return checkout handoff unless an integrated purchase provider is configured. Autonomous purchase is only available within a confirmed quote through an integrated provider such as Hermes, OpenClaw, Dizko Checkout, a partner API, or delegated payment.

Do not confirm a purchase unless the event, venue, date, quantity, ticket type, max total, and refund constraints are correct. If price, date, venue, ticket type, quantity, or terms change, the assistant should stop and ask again.
